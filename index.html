Your first visit

What to expect,
step by step.

Most new patients get their first treatment the same day.

  1. Brief paperwork

    A few forms about you and your condition. Fill them out online before you come to save time.

  2. Consultation

    Sit down with your doctor to talk through what's bothering you, your concerns and your options.

  3. History & exam

    A few questions and specialized tests to find out exactly which tissues are affected.

  4. X-rays, if needed

    Only when your condition calls for it, to rule out anything serious and plan the right care.

  5. Same-day treatment

    Usually your first treatment happens right then: adjustments, physical therapies or soft tissue work.

  6. Your plan for home

    Simple instructions like ice or heat and stretches, plus a follow-up within a day or two to review findings.
