**Description** (4,000 max)

Shift Score turns your shift into a live paycheck. Clock in and watch your earnings climb by the second, level up as the hours stack, and know exactly what you've made before payday.

LIVE PAY COUNTER
• See today's earnings tick up in real time, to the cent
• Unpaid breaks pause the counter automatically
• Level up through your shift and unlock badges like "Coffee Covered" and "Full Day"
• A reminder if you've been clocked in for 9 hours, so a forgotten clock-out doesn't pad your hours

A TIMESHEET THAT FILLS ITSELF IN
• Every clock-in and clock-out lands in a clean, week-by-week timesheet
• Clock back in later the same day and it adds to that day's shift
• Fix a start or end time in a tap
• Export to Excel with working formulas

KNOW YOUR PAYCHECK
• Set how often you're paid: weekly, every two weeks, twice a month, monthly or daily
• See each pay period's hours, gross pay and take-home estimate
• Take-home estimates include Social Security, Medicare, federal income tax and your state's tax
• Add your real pay stubs and estimates get more accurate over time

REPORTS READY TO HAND IN
• Timesheets by day, week, pay period, month or any dates you choose
• Includes breaks, totals, the math behind them and signature lines
• Share as a Word document or Excel file

A BUDGET THAT SORTS ITSELF
• Set monthly or per-paycheck limits for food, groceries, gas, bills, fun and your own categories
• Purchases are sorted into categories automatically as you type
• See what's left, how much you can spend per day, and whether you're on pace to go over
• Get a budget suggested from your actual pay

SEE WHERE YOUR MONEY GOES
• Note where you bought something, in person or online
• Pin a store's location with one tap, or save the website
• See your top spots and how much goes online versus in person
• Track your balance and paydays

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account and no sign-in
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Your hours and money stay on your phone. Back up to a file whenever you like.

Shift Score is for anyone paid by the hour: students, part-timers, baristas, retail and restaurant staff, front-desk teams, interns and freelancers.

Pay and tax figures are estimates, not tax advice. Your employer's records and pay stubs are the official numbers.

**App Review notes** (App Store Connect → App Review Information)
No login is required. To test: enter any job name and hourly rate on the welcome screen, tap Clock In on the Today tab and watch the live counter. Tap Clock Out to save the shift, then see it on the Timesheet tab. Location is optional: it's requested only if you tap "Here" when logging a purchase, and is stored on the device. Native features: on-device reminder notifications while clocked in (permission is requested at first clock-in), haptic feedback on clock in and out, and the share sheet for exporting Excel and Word reports and backups. All data is stored on the device. The app makes no network requests for user data.

**App Privacy** (App Store Connect → App Privacy)
Choose **"No, we do not collect data from this app."** Result: "Data Not Collected."

**Export compliance:** Already answered in the app (it uses no non-exempt encryption), so you won't be asked on each upload.
