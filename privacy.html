Shift Score

Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

The short version: Shift Score keeps your hours, pay and spending on your own device. There's no account, no server holding your data, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never see, sell or share your information.

What the app stores

To work, Shift Score saves the details you enter: your hourly rate, clock-in and clock-out times, breaks, pay schedule, take-home and tax settings (such as your state and filing status), spending, deposits and balance notes, and display preferences. All of it is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. Shift Score has no sign-in and does not collect, transmit or receive your personal information. It includes no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules an on-device reminder (for example, "Still on the clock?") while you're clocked in. These reminders are created on your phone. No notification server is involved.

Files you export

When you export a spreadsheet, report or backup, the app creates the file on your device and opens your phone's share sheet. Where the file goes from there (Files, email, Messages and so on) is your choice and is governed by that service's own policies.

The website version

Shift Score is also available as a website hosted on GitHub Pages. The site works the same way: your data stays in your browser's storage on your device. GitHub, as the host, may process basic technical information such as IP addresses to deliver the site, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you're always in control. Use Settings → Erase all data in the app, or delete the app, to remove everything. We can't recover data for you, so export a backup first if you might want it later.

Children

Shift Score is a general-audience tool and is not directed to children under 13. Since the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect any from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
